# Antibody Design

## Overview
AI-driven computational antibody design spanning structure-based and sequence-based approaches. Includes use of Boltzgen for nanobody/binder generation, IgFold for structure prediction, CDR identification, sequence similarity search, and multi-model consensus strategies. Core research area for Actimo Labs.
